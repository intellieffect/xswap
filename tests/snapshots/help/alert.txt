usage: xswap alert [-h] [--install] [--uninstall] [--status] [--dry-run]
                   [--warn PCT] [--every MINUTES] [--cached SECONDS]
                   [--auto-switch]

options:
  -h, --help        show this help message and exit
  --install         Write the wrapper script and plist, then load it
  --uninstall       Unload the job and delete the wrapper script and plist
  --status          Show whether the job is installed and loaded, and the last
                    log tail
  --dry-run         Print what --install/--uninstall would do without changing
                    anything
  --warn PCT        Threshold passed to `list --warn` (1-100, default 15)
  --every MINUTES   Polling interval in whole minutes (default 30; launchd
                    merges under 60s)
  --cached SECONDS  Freshness passed to `list --cached` (default 600)
  --auto-switch     With --install: run `xswap auto-tick --cached SECONDS`
                    before the warn step and post a notification when it
                    switches
